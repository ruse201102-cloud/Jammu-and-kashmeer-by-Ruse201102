Srinagar

Srinagar is the summer capital of Jammu and Kashmir and is famous for its beautiful Dal Lake, traditional houseboats and Mughal gardens. Visitors can enjoy Shikara rides, explore local markets and experience the unique culture of Kashmir.

Famous Attractions

  • Dal Lake
  • Shalimar Bagh
  • Nishat Bagh
  • Shankaracharya Temple

Gulmarg

Gulmarg is a beautiful hill station known for its snow-covered mountains, green meadows and breathtaking views. It is especially popular for skiing during winter and the famous Gulmarg Gondola cable car.

Famous Attractions

  • Gulmarg Gondola
  • Khilanmarg
  • Golf Course
  • St. Mary's Church
Back to Top

Pahalgam

Pahalgam is a peaceful town surrounded by pine forests, mountains and the Lidder River. It is a popular destination for nature lovers, trekking and sightseeing.

Famous Attractions

  • Betaab Valley
  • Aru Valley
  • Chandanwari
  • Lidder River
Back to Top

Sonamarg

Sonamarg, meaning "Meadow of Gold", is famous for its glaciers, mountain landscapes and beautiful valleys. It offers spectacular views and is a popular starting point for several mountain excursions.

Famous Attractions

  • Thajiwas Glacier
  • Zero Point
  • Vishansar Lake
  • Sind River
Back to Top

Patnitop

Patnitop is a scenic hill station in the Jammu region, surrounded by forests and mountain views. It is known for its pleasant weather, peaceful surroundings and outdoor activities.

Famous Attractions

  • Nathatop
  • Sanasar Lake
  • Skyview Patnitop
  • Kud
Back to Top

Vaishno Devi

Vaishno Devi is one of India's most visited pilgrimage destinations. The sacred shrine is located in the Trikuta Mountains near Katra. Pilgrims undertake a mountain journey to visit the holy cave shrine.

Famous Attractions

  • Vaishno Devi Bhawan
  • Ardhkuwari Temple
  • Himkoti
  • Bhairon Temple
Back to Top

Doodhpathri

Doodhpathri is a picturesque meadow surrounded by green hills and forests. Its name means "Valley of Milk". The destination is known for its peaceful atmosphere, flowing streams and scenic beauty.

Famous Attractions

  • Tangnar
  • Shaliganga River
  • Doodhpathri Meadows
Back to Top

Aru Valley

Aru Valley is a beautiful mountain village near Pahalgam. It is surrounded by lush green meadows, forests and snow-covered peaks. It is popular for trekking, camping and enjoying nature.

Famous Attractions

  • Aru Meadows
  • Lidderwat
  • Kolahoi Glacier Trek
